# Spec: Meals

Status: draft · Upstream checked: 2.83.0

## Purpose

The household's week plan as a native screen, at parity with `public/pages/meals.js`: navigate
weeks, plan meals per day and meal type, edit and delete them, manage their ingredients, plan
recipes, fill the week at random, and send ingredients to a shopping list.

## Requirements (draft)

- MEALS-R1 week plan in the household's week start and visible meal types.
- MEALS-R2 add, edit, delete a meal (title, type, date, cook, recipe, notes, link, weekly repeat).
- MEALS-R3 ingredients add, edit, delete.
- MEALS-R4 title suggestions, random plan (apply-plan), recipe rail.
- MEALS-R5 meal and week to shopping list with undo.
- MEALS-R6 read-only, offline, errors.
